## Open: known gaps and housekeeping

- Before alpha.5, Media's Source/Queue workers could outlive Rainmeter and block upgrades. Copies at alpha.4 or earlier still have this. alpha.5 and later fix it, but the first upgrade from an old copy may need Rainmeter quit and those `powershell.exe` workers ended by hand ([UPDATES.md](UPDATES.md#upgrading-past-running-media-helpers)).
- A Windows PowerShell helper that starts using `Get-FileHash` or a `Microsoft.PowerShell.Security` cmdlet needs the same `PSModulePath` reset as `UpdateInstall.ps1`.
- Three Global Settings test fixtures were stale before developer mode: `Test-ThemeDropdown.ps1`, `Test-SettingsFields.ps1` and `Test-SettingsRefresh.ps1` still expected the 758 px panel and did not stage `Version.inc`, `Update\*` or `Idle\Link.lua`, so none could run since the Idle pause and Updates sections. On 2026-10-28 their file lists and heights were brought up to date; the first two pass, and `Test-SettingsRefresh.ps1` (run it with `powershell.exe`, not PowerShell 7) now stops at "Scale burst: pixels changed outside MeterGeometry/MeterScaleInput (row 761)", a row inside the Updates section that its before/after capture comparison does not allow for. It predates the Developer section (which starts below that row) and is still open.
- Four developer-only native checks that failed or flaked on 0.1.0-alpha.14 were fixed on 2026-11-24 (Rainmeter 4.5.28.3936, each run alone under Windows PowerShell 5.1). Every cause was in a fixture: no product fault was found and no product file changed.
  - `Test-MediaSettings.ps1 -IdentityFocused` still expected neutral song, artist and album icons (Accent 1 since 2026-11-19, [decision C5](superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-media-queue-art-and-album-colors-design.md)) and, hidden behind that, near-black transport glyphs (white since 2026-10-25). It passes 3 of 3 (25 layouts, 6 captures), as do `-IdentityIconsFocused`, `-PlayerNameFocused` and `-PlayerNameIconsFocused`; muted icons, a blank song icon and dark glyphs each make it fail.
  - `Test-IdleStandDown.ps1 -Skin Media,Setup` loaded album colors without the Chameleon plugin; they are now stubbed, as in the Media harnesses (a one-off run with the real DLL in the isolated instance also passed, 28 of 28). It also compared the spectrum mask's divider with its value before the pause, but that divider follows the output's signal: with music playing it reads 1 before, -1 on the first tick of the resume's reload and 1 again 3 s later. The mask is now checked against the capture state at all three points, which catches a signal action that no longer restores it. The repeats showed a third, intermittent fault: the runner's poll of `CaptureState.inc` locked out Rainmeter's own `!WriteKeyValue`; it now reads shared (polling as fast as possible, the old read fails 3 of 3 and the new one passes 3 of 3). With music playing it then passed 4 of 4 at 220/1, and with silent output once (42 checks), as did the standalone Visualizer (27). 180/0.75 and a combined Chronometer, Media and Visualizer run passed once each, with music, before that last change.
  - `Test-MediaCaptureLease.ps1` stage A judged variant 1 with the PC's real input, so it failed whenever the keyboard or mouse had been used in the last 5 minutes: a paused drawer resumes at the coordinator's first sync, as designed. Its idle input is now staged (56 checks, 0 failures). Stage B needs silent output, because the lease parks only after quiet: the unchanged stage passes 39 of 39 with music playing once a scratch skin's idle floor counts that music as quiet, so sound is the whole cause. It now stops with a `SOUND` line that says so, before the first park or in any later one, and the run ends as not judged instead of failing step after step. Stage C passes (13 checks). With silent output (peak 0.0000 throughout) the unchanged stage B passes 39 of 39 and the new one 39 of 39 twice; stages A and C pass there too.
  - `Test-MediaTransport.ps1` timed out when the runner had the report open as the observer replaced it. The observer now tries again on its next update and reports how often it had to. With the report deliberately held open, the old observer fails 2 of 2 and the new one passes 3 of 3 (16 to 21 retries); 10 of 10 ordinary runs pass (no retry needed).
  - No other harness asserts a delete-and-rename report. `Run-UpdateSmoke.ps1`'s probe deletes and renames without asserting, on every update, and is read only inside the same Rainmeter. Network's `QA/SettingsSmoke.lua` asserts that it can delete the runner's command file, a similar race that has not been seen to fail and was left alone.
- **First placement has a smallest screen.** All ten panels are apart from 1596 × 790 up at the default size, the eight utilities and Welcome from 1140 × 790, the eight utilities from 1140 × 651. A 1080p screen at 125% or 150% Windows scaling is below the first ([WELCOME.md](WELCOME.md#what-fits)). It is in the README's Known issues. Panels an older version left at 0,0 stay there until Arrange (on main).
- **Media settings is taller than a small screen**, the only settings panel left that is at the default suite scale (Thread colors joins it above 40 threads, Disk settings above 7 drive rows). It is 850 px as shipped and 934 with the queue expanded (28 px more since the Spectrum position row; computed from the layout, not re-measured), against a work area of 816 px on a 1080p screen at 125% Windows scaling and 672 at 150%. At 150% the queue's Start, Stop and Restart, the Connections link and the Tools & help row are below the screen; at 125% the Tools & help row is, with the queue expanded, and the shipped panel is now 34 px over too. Which rows fall below has not been re-measured since that row was added. The other tall panels scroll since 2026-11-25 ([how to add it](UTILITY-SETTINGS.md#using-it-in-another-panel)); Media's files were being changed by another task that day. No scroll is planned for it: it fits both of the owner's screens and 1080p at 100%. It is in the README's Known issues.
- **The short-screen scroll was checked with simulated work areas only**, except for CPU settings on the laptop's own 1019 px. No wheel, touchpad or touch screen was used by hand, and no 1080p screen at 125% or 150% was seen ([what that does not prove](UTILITY-SETTINGS.md#checks-2026-11-25)).
